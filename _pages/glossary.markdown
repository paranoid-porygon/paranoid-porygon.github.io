I might use uncommon phrases, technical terms, slang, or shorthand on occassion that need defining or explaining. This is where that'll happen.

## Abbreviations

**DNF** - "did not finish"; used to denote when a reader failed or declined to read a book all the way through

**WWSD** - "What would Stoner do?", in reference to Eugene Stoner who invented the AR-15/M-16 rifle; a concept rifle cooked up by Karl Kasarda, Ian McCullum, and Russel Phagan of InRange TV fame which inclusdes a pencil barrel, carbon fiber handguard, chrome-lined bolt carrier group, an upper receiver with no forward assist, a monolithic polymer lower receiver, and a caputred buffer system, all of which in the interest of creating a lightweight, no-frills, but practical general purpose rifle

## Key terms

**Anocracy** - the dangerous interstitial position on a sliding scale that has democracy at one end and autocracy at the other

**Big Tech** - large market capitalization companies who manufactures technology --primarily but not exclusively software and cloud services-- that have an outsized influence in commerce, finance, communications, culture, and public policy; e.g. Facebook, Amazon, Oracle, Palantir, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Twitter, TikTok, Microsoft, Uber, Spotify, Tesla

**Enshittification** - the gradual process of online (usually free) services/platforms degrading due to monetization after having achieved critical user mass and eliminating all meaningful competitive alternatives

### Neologisms

**Clintonmaxxing** - attempting to return to the ostensible peaceful times of the Clinton era by living like we did in the '90s: offline and outside, minimizing ubiquitous computing's stranglehold on our lives and our attention

**Dealgorithming** - the act of extricating oneself from algorithmically generated recommender systems or feeds, or the more general act of divesting from Big Tech's ecosystem of services and apps

**Far-Centrism** - similar to Radical Centrism, but instead of treating moderation as a virtue until itself, *Far-Centrism* is the valorization of remaining "apolitical" even beyond the point of reason or good sense
