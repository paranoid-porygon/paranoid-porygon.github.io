I might use uncommon phrases, technical terms, slang, or shorthand on occassion that need defining or explaining. This is where that'll happen.

## Abbreviations

**DNF** - "did not finish"; used to denote when a reader failed or declined to read a book all the way through

## Key terms

**Big Tech** - large market capitalization companies who manufactures technology --primarily but not exclusively software and cloud services-- that have an outsized influence in commerce, finance, communications, culture, and public policy; e.g. Facebook, Amazon, Oracle, Palantir, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Twitter, TikTok, Microsoft, Uber, Spotify, Tesla

**Enshittification** - the gradual process of online (usually free) services/platforms degrading due to monetization after having achieved critical user mass and eliminating all meaningful competitive alternatives

### Neologisms

**Dealgorithming** - the act of extricating oneself from algorithmically generated recommender systems or feeds, or the more general act of divesting from Big Tech's ecosystem of services and apps
