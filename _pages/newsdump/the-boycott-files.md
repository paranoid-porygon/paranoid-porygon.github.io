# Overview of Ongoing Boycotts and Related Updates

Whenever I run across an ongoing boycott that seems worth considering, I will share the subject, reason, goal, and current status on this page.

# Any game developer or publisher that uses kernel-level anti-cheat software

Kernel-level access enables some nasty malware possibilities, and that sort of access should be tightly restricted. If you are aware of the [Intel Management Engine vulnerability](), this is a similar idea except that it has already been exploited in the wild[1]()[2]() and creates an even broader attack surface. I have personally seen gaming companies remotely access their software installed on the computers of suspected bad actors; this is not just a theoretical vulnerability. 

## Goal

Gamers should demand that game software, including anti-cheat, only get Ring-3 access and exist in userspace just like any other user-facing software. Gamers should also then take steps to sandbox software installed on their computers using Mandatory Access Control, and they should further consider isolating their gaming devices on their home networks using logical or physical network segmentation.

# Airbnb, VRBO, and short term residential home rentals

*Last Updated: 2025-10-24*

I personally boycott Airbnb, VRBO, or any service or organization that turns what would otherwise be residential homes into short-term rentals. There is nothing that these companies can do to persuade me to give them my business; I think they are ontologically immoral and wasteful and actively enable people to become petty landlords.

## Reason

Land is scarce and the demand for homes currently exceeds the supply in the United States[[1](https://realestate.usnews.com/real-estate/housing-market-index/articles/housing-market-predictions-for-the-next-5-years)]. Hoarding homes meant for family ownership exacerbates this scarcity[[2](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/over-5-million-semi-vacant-185618591.html)] and contributes to high home prices. In any other context, the accumulation of basic necessities at the expense of others in need would be referred to as gluttony. This is grotesque behavior and should not be rewarded.

## Goal

Condo and Homeowners Associations need to ban property owners from cosplaying as hotels in their neighborhoods.

Travelers should rent rooms from established hotels that make much more efficient use of the land that they are built on (and don't force you to do chores before you leave and charge a gratuitous fee if you don't!)

# Target

*Last Updated: 2025-10-24*

Target is currently subject to a nationwide boycott over its reversal of social justice commitments that it had made in 2020 and 2021. 

I am personally participating in this boycott. This is a fairly easy boycott to participate in: anything I can get at Target I can get elsewhere, often at lower prices. Additionally, the demands for this boycott are pretty meager and reasonable for Target to fulfill.

## Reason

In the aftermath of the reelection of Donald Trump as president of the United States, several companies slashed DEI (Diversity, Equity, and Inclusivity) initiatives. Target was one such company and as a result became the subject of a nationwide boycott, spearheaded by pastor Jamal Bryant[[1](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/classroom/daily-news-lessons/2025/08/pastor-leading-target-boycott-on-its-impact-and-the-retailers-response)].

The most generous explanation for Target's decision is that they feared retribution from the Trump administration which has threatened to pursue "discrimination lawsuits" against companies engaging in DEI initiatives.

## Goal

In 2020, Target committed to increasing its Black staff by 20% over three years and invest $10 million in social justice organizations. In 2021, they pledged to dedicate $2 billion towards Black-owned businesses before the end of 2025. In January 2025, Target stopped pursuing the aforementioned goals it hadd set.

Boycott organizers want those decisions reverted and for Target to meet the goals it had originally set for itself.

## Status

Target has experienced a slump in sales, however it does not appear to be a result of the boycott. The number of Americans who regularly shop at Target has declined 19% since 2021. There does appear to have been a tradeoff in who exactly shops at Target and the change tacks to party lines[[2](https://apnews.com/article/target-walmart-ceo-boycott-dei-7996ce3fbf7f0cc9207472bc7a227cd6)].
