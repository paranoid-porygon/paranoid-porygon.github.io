# This Site

The content of this site will mostly be updated as blog posts; these posts will probably be, but aren't limited to, updates on hobbies, op-eds, interesting articles I read, and media reviews. If I create any technical documentation, I might add it as a static link on the sidebar.

I opted for a static site because it's easier to maintain and can easily be rendered on any browser, including text-only terminal browsers and ones that do not support JavaScript. This design philosophy was inspired by the Viewable With Any Browser campaign and The Best Page in the Universe. This site is built using the Jekyll framework with no plugins, which I hope to stick to. I chose the goldenrod text and black background because, in my testing, this combination is the least straining on readers' eyes.

This site is optimally viewed on a desktop or laptop computer screen. I do not have any plans to optimize the site layout for mobile devices; I think ubiquitous computing is bad and having your neck craned and face glued to a screen in public is unseemly, so I do not wish to enable that.

## Why {#this-site-why}

My goal is for this site to replace my various social media pages and profiles. The companies and individuals who run these sites have proven themselves to be capricious and arbitrary in moderating the content on their sites, often punishing legitimate, valid discourse or censoring inoffensive content, while bad actors and opportunists spread disinformation and brain-dead slop. Their business models are diametrically opposed to user privacy and data security. Several have engaged in undisclosed psychological experiments on their users without their consent. Some examples: 

* Facebook's moderator AI gave me a warning for posting a comic that _mocked_ neo-nazis and Lost Causers simply because it depicted a swastika, and their appeal board is staffed by underpaid people likely outside of the US who didn't have the cultural context to understand what the comic was saying
* Twitter, upon acquisition by Musk, blocks users from posting tweets that contain the word "cisgender" but not words like "retarded"
* YouTube continually revises, rescinds, and even retroactively applies their rules regarding completely legal and educational, non-advertisement firearms-related content all while their recommender algorithms continue to push illicit, and often violent and bigoted, content to users, including minors
* TikTok has made it increasingly difficult to use their platform from a web browser, pressuring users to install their app which requires microphone, camera, and location access to simply scroll your feed (not even make new videos!)
* Social media has repeatedly been demonstrated to have deleterious effects on mental health, especially in children, and the people running these sites are fully aware of this fact which is why they do not allow their own children to use them
* Facebook engaged in a secret experiment to see if they could manipulate users' moods depending on what they were shown in their feeds
* Google actively surveils users through their Chrome products while shuttering useful services and open source projectst and firing prominent ethicists

There are several decentralized social networks currently in development and gaining popularity, namely BlueSky and, my favorite, Mastodon. These are a step in the right direction, however I still want my own piece of the web where I have complete authorial control.

# Me

I took some precautions to make this site reasonably anonymized such that if someone were to stumble across this site, they wouldn't be able to run a reverse-DNS or domain search to reveal my identity; I used an alias and a Privacy Card to purchase this domain. That said, my personal friends know that this is my site so, at the end of the day, they could dox me if they wanted to. **Please don't.**
