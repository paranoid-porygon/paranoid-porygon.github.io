This is my website.

I opted for a static site because it's easier to maintain and can easily be rendered on any browser, including text-only terminal browsers and ones that do not support JavaScript. This design philosophy was inspired by the Viewable With Any Browser campaign and The Best Page in the Universe.

The content of this site will mostly be updated as blog posts; these posts will probably be, but aren't limited to, updates on hobbies, op-eds, interesting articles I read, and media reviews. If I create any technical documentation, I might add it as a static link on the sidebar.

My goal is for this site to replace my various social media pages and profiles. The companies and individuals who run these sites have proven themselves to be capricious and arbitrary in moderating the content on their sites, often punishing legitimate, valid discourse or censoring inoffensive content, while bad actors and opportunists spread disinformation and brain-dead slop. Some examples: 

* Facebook's moderator AI gave me a warning for posting a comic that _mocked_ neo-nazis simply because it depicted a swastika, and their appeal board is staffed by underpaid people likely outside of the US who didn't have the cultural context to understand what the comic was saying
* Twitter, upon acquisition by Musk, blocks users from posting tweets that contain the word "cisgender" but not words like "retarded"
* YouTube continually revises, rescinds, and even retroactively applies their rules regarding firearms-related content while their recommender algorithms continue to push illicit, and often violent and bigoted, content to users, including minors
* Social media has repeatedly been demonstrated to have deliterious effects on mental health, especially in children
* TikTok has made it increasingly difficult to use their platform from a web browser, demanding that users use their app which requires microphone, camera, and location access to simply scroll your feed (not even make new videos!)

There are several decentralized social networks currently in development and gaining popularity, namely BlueSky and, my favorite, Mastodon. These are a step in the right direction, however I still want my own piece of the web where I have complete authorial control.
