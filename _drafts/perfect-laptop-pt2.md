I had [written previously](/blog/laptop-wishlist) about what qualities I would want in a laptop if I could design one. The condensed version is that I wanted to see the Intel Management Engine disabled like in Leah Rowe's [Minifree][minifree] Thinkpads, the materials and sleek aesthetics of a MacBook, the form factor of a Chomebook (if not even smaller), and the freedom to repair like what is offered by Framework. I should have done a bit more research before publishing that post because that exact laptop exists --or rather, existed-- in the form of the Star Lite MkIV from Star Labs.
