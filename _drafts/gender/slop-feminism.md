Feminism had a bit of a moment sometime between the launch of Tumblr and the #MeToo movement, but like most critical frameworks it became tainted by [pernicious libertarianism](glossary#pervasive-libertarianism) the moment it entered the discourse on Web 2.0. Largely due to the broader palatability of liberal feminism over other strains (particularly the more academic or radical ones), its concerns and definition were reduced to "the sexes should be treated equally". To this brand of feminism's credit, it did popularize intersectionality, in particular elevating the struggles of trans people and shoving [TERF](glossary#terf)S to the fringes. But something was lost in the process of trying to memory hole bygone radical feminism: a healthy skepticism of, if not wholesale opposition to, pornography in particular and sex work more broadly, and recognition of their contribution to cultural misogyny.
