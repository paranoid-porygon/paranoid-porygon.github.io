# Arguments

* SAC is optimistic that people can and will retain their individuality in a hyperconnected environment; Lain posits that people constitute a collective consciousness and progress in communications, networks, and cybernetics will further enmesh people together
* Despite the superficial "collectivism vs. individualism" nature of this dichotomy, digging into the actual points of view of the shows (and their fandoms) reveals that SAC is actually left-coded --or at least libertarian-coded-- and Lain is right-coded
* SAC, especially in its second season, makes a case for multicultural liberalism and the coexistence of distinct individuals in a hyperconnected space
* Lain, like the philosophies that inspired it, is more nihilistic.
