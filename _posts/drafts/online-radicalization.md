If you are a parent of a child under the age of 24, especially young men or boys, it is of vital importance that you take an active role in minimizing your child's access to extremist and radicalizing content on the web if they are under 18, and to intervene if you detect extreme or antisocial behavior or ideologies if they are already legal adults. 

As children, they do not have the maturity, knowledge, or moral framework to navigate the unfiltered, uncurated internet. They do not possess the education or information context to properly discern truth from disinformation, propaganda, or intentional manipulation. Platforms like YouTube, Discord, Twitter, TikTok, or Instagram do not have the capacity to reliably filter all the content posted to their sites/apps to verify age appropriateness, let alone remove or provide warnings on content uploaded by bad actors.
