The Trump DOJ is *finally* doing what was obviously in the pipe: attempting to [ban trans folks from being able to purchase firearms on the grounds that they are "mentally defective"][doj-trans-gun-ban]. Since the [attack on the Covenant School in Nashville][nashville], right-wing pundits completely abandoned any pretense of being pro-gun absolutists and [openly][3] [called][4] [for][5] expanding the bannable criteria found in the ATF Form 4473 to include being transgender, transexual, genderqueer, or otherwise not identifying with the M or the F on your birth certificate. Now with a second Reichstag Fire event in the form of the Annunciation Catholic Church shooting, the Trump administration sees an opportunity to answer that call. 

To be abundantly clear: the overwhelming majority of mass shootings are committed by cisgender males under the age of 24 who are usually white, and not just the majority but [white males are disporportionately represented amongst mass shooters][shooters-by-race-and-gender]. Meanwhile, trans people make up somewhere around 0.5% of the total US population and make up disproportionately fewer as a portion of mass shooting perpetrators. **There is no apparent pattern of trans people commiting more mass shootings than anyone else.** [There *is* a pattern of cisgender males committing more mass shootings][7], with 96% of mass shooters being male despite men only making up 47% of the US population, but even still the occurrence of mass shootings are statistically rare such that nobody considers it reasonable to conduct targeted gun gans against whites, hispanics, or men. So it would be even more ridiculous to propose such a ban on trans people.

We are still in the early stages of fact-finding about the attack on Annunciation, however some preliminary evidence suggests that the perpetrator may have been involved in online nihilistic and [Black Pill][black-pill]-adjacent groups, and that those groups pressured the assailant into detransitioning. None of this would be particularly surprising; researchers following extremism and youth crime are well-aware of these trends, and even when a perpetrator doesn't fully fit the typical profile, they still usually end up having been part of [these communities][mass-shooter-communities].

I do not normally suggest impulse purchasing or panic buying anything, especially a firearm, but we exist amidst extenuating circumstances that look an awful lot like the early stages of a pogrom. If you are trans and if you have never purchased or even used a firearm before, consider purchasing a semi-automatic rifle or PCC (pistol caliber carbine) very soon. You should strongly consider an AR-pattern rifle or PCC over any other alternatives; they are widely available and compatible parts are abundant. Handguns are more difficult to use, especially under duress or if you have little training or are not particularly physically adept; stocked weapons like rifles are much easier to use. If you live in a state that allows private sales without the need to fill out a 4473 at a gun store, consider making a private purchase. Sites like [Outdoors Trader][odt] work as classified ads for private firearm sales. As long as you are not otherwise banned from owning a firearm, such as if you are a violent felon or currently using drugs outlawed at the federal level (yes, that includes marijuana), this is perfectly legal. You may want to consider boymoding (or brining a friend/partner if not possible) when you meet the seller to ensure your safety, and always make sure to meet in a public place. **DO NOT get someone else to make the transaction on your behalf or under their name if you're the intended purchaser of a firearm; this is what is known as a straw purchase and is a federal crime, and it is a jailable offense.**

Once you have acquired a rifle/PCC, reach out to a local chapter of a queer-friendly gun org. Examples include [Pink Pistols][pink-pistols], [Rainbow Reload][rainbow-reload], [The Liberal Gun Club][liberal-gun-club], and the [Socialist Rifle Association][sra], in descending order of preference. The will put you in touch with trustworthy trainers who should be able to teach you not only how to shoot but also the self-defense laws of your state, basic trauma first aid, Don't Be A Victim training, on top of providing a safe community.

I also want to make a plea to trans folks to stop using Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and Twitter. The owners of these sites are [openly hostile][zuck] [to trans folks][musk], and in the past have given information to law enforcement without a signed warrant. The same goes for Amazon smarthome devices, including the Ring security camera ecosystem. To my knowledge, Google has been slightly less willing to accomodate sans a signed warrant, however there's no guarantee that will always be the case. Be incredibly careful about what you post online; even if you don't do so under your real name, you can still be identified in a variety of ways if you aren't particularly well-versed in preserving your internet anonymity. Most VPN providers are a scam whose goal is to harvest your data, and even the single reputable one (Mullvad) isn't a magic bullet for privacy or anonymity. Maintain a variety of aliases, be careful what you post, try to avoid links between accounts or shared passwords, and do your best to mask your IP and device fingerprint.
