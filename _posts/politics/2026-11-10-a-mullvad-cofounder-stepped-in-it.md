One of the cofounders of Mullvad VPN, a product that I use and have consistently recommended to people as an unambiguously more privacy-conscious alternative to [competitors][cape-technologies], donated so much money to a [post-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-conservatism) Swedish political party that in 2025 it was able to field candidates in their national elections for the first time. This [royally](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48717469) [pissed off](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/donation-controversy) a large portion of the Mullvad userbase, primarily the libertarian, liberal, and broadly leftward end of that userbase: they see the [Örebro Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96rebro_Party) as not-very-vaguely fascist and fascism as intrinsically opposed to personal privacy, as it requires a high level of surveillance to achieve its goals. Unfortunately there's one other political group who greatly values personal privacy: violent extremists, especially those of the racist, anti-immigrant, and anti-queer varieties; I anticipate that the cofounder's actions *and* the subsequent furor after they became public knowledge will only attract more bigoted users to the service (though perhaps not enough to make up for lost revenue).

<!--excerpt-->

I'm not going to tell you that you should boycott Mullvad because, honestly, there isn't a good alternative. ToR and I2P are decentralized and volunteer-run and bypass the problem of BDFLs spending their money in reprehensible ways, but they are slow as ass. Other VPNs either spy on you much more overtly, or just aren't technologically up-to-snuff. 

Additionally, I don't know how much harm is being done by continuing to be a patron of Mullvad compared to the benefits that many at-risk individuals receive by using it. The OP currently holds zero seats in the Swedish Riksdag and a small minority of seats in its home county and municipality in Sweden; its influence is growing in its home region, however. It's a similar dilemma faced by current/prospective Tesla owners: are you doing more harm by giving Elon Musk money than you are reducing harm by getting an electric vehicle? At least with Tesla, there are **many** viable alternatives.

I briefly skimmed the [blog](https://dberntsson.info/2026/07/20/immigration/) of the infringing cofounder, and frankly the guy does not strike me as a particularly deep thinker. He seems to have the political instincts of a teenager who discovered Ayn Rand for the first time. Typical techbro gobbledygook that someone who doesn't know better might find profound.

I'll leave to the reader to decide if it's worth cancelling their Mullvad service over. Personally, I plan to keep using it because I think the benefits outweigh the harms, for now. When that changes, or if a compelling alternative presents itself, I'll make a switch.
