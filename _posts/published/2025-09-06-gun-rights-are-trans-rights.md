The Trump DOJ is finally doing what was obviously in the pipe: attempting to [ban trans folks from being able to purchase firearms on the grounds that they are "mentally defective"][doj-trans-gun-ban]. Since the [attack on the Covenant School in Nashville][nashville], right-wing pundits completely abandoned any pretense of being pro-gun absolutists and [openly][3] [called][4] [for][5] expanding the bannable criteria found in the ATF Form 4473 to include being transgender, transexual, genderqueer, or otherwise not identifying with the M or the F on your birth certificate. Now with a second Reichstag Fire event in the form of the Annunciation Catholic Church shooting, the Trump administration sees an opportunity to answer that call. 

Trans people make up somewhere around 0.5% of the total US population and make up disproportionately fewer as a portion of mass shooting perpetrators. **There is no apparent pattern of trans people commiting more mass shootings than anyone else.** [There *is* a pattern of cisgender males committing more mass shootings][7], with 96% of mass shooters being male despite men only making up 47% of the US population, but even still the occurrence of mass shootings are statistically rare such that nobody considers it reasonable to conduct targeted gun gans against whites, hispanics, or men. So it would be even more ridiculous to propose such a ban on trans people.

![mass-shootings-by-gender](/assets/post-images/gun-rights-are-trans-rights/piechart.jpg){:width="80ch"}

We are still in the early stages of fact-finding about the attack on Annunciation, however some preliminary evidence suggests that the perpetrator may have been involved in [online nihilistic groups][black-pill], and that those groups [pressured the assailant into detransitioning][detrans]. None of this would be particularly surprising; researchers following extremism and youth crime are well-aware of these trends, and even when a perpetrator doesn't fully fit the typical profile, they still usually end up having been part of [these communities][mass-shooter-communities].

Gun rights orgs in the US, including the [NRA][nra], have already condemned the DOJ's attempt to strip Second Amendment rights from citizens without due process, compelling case that it's in the interests of the public, or legal precedent. Categorically denying a Constitutionally-protected right will not fly once challeneged in federal court, similarly to Trump's [attempt to end birthright citizenship][birthright-citizenship], though even that is still an open question. And considering that ICE is operating outside its stated mandate of only going after violent criminals, and in fact nabbing [legal permanent residents][permanent-residents] and even [citizens][citizen] and terrorizing bystanders in the process, it's not unreasonable to expect the ATF to be given similar leeway. 

I do not normally suggest impulse purchasing or panic buying anything, especially a firearm, but we exist amidst extenuating circumstances that look an awful lot like the early stages of a pogrom. If you are trans and if you have never purchased or even used a firearm before, consider purchasing a [semi-automatic rifle][AR-15] or [PCC (pistol caliber carbine)][EP-9] very soon. You should strongly consider an AR-pattern rifle or PCC over any other alternatives; they are widely available and compatible parts are abundant. Handguns are more difficult to use, especially under duress or if you have little training or are not particularly physically adept; stocked weapons like rifles are much easier to use. If you live in a state that allows private sales without the need to fill out a 4473 at a gun store, consider making a private purchase. Sites like [Outdoors Trader][odt] work as classified ads for private firearm sales. As long as you are not otherwise banned from owning a firearm, such as if you are a violent felon or currently using drugs outlawed at the federal level (yes, that includes marijuana), this is perfectly legal. You may want to consider boymoding (or brining a friend/partner if not possible) when you meet the seller to ensure your safety, and always make sure to meet in a public place. **DO NOT get someone else to make the transaction on your behalf or under their name if you're the intended purchaser of a firearm; this is what is known as a straw purchase and is a federal crime, and it is a jailable offense.**

Once you have acquired a rifle/PCC, reach out to a local chapter of a queer-friendly gun org. Examples include [Pink Pistols][pink-pistols], [The Liberal Gun Club][liberal-gun-club], and the [Socialist Rifle Association][sra], in descending order of preference. They will put you in touch with trustworthy trainers who should be able to teach you not only how to shoot but also the self-defense laws of your state, basic trauma first aid, Don't Be A Victim training, on top of providing a safe community.

Be extremely skeptical of any information you find online suggesting you make one gun-related purchase or another. Oftentimes influencers are paid by manufacturers to shill products that aren't really that good, are overpriced, or are unnecessary gimmicks. The most reliable sources of gun information I have found are [PewPew Tactical][ppt], [InRangeTV][irtv], and [Yellow Peril Tactical][ypt]. All these sources will make the same gun recommendation: **just get an AR-15 and a Glock 19**.

I am actively putting together a much more comprehensive [first time gun buyer's guide][first-gun-guide]. It is still a work-in-progress but at time of writing is mostly complete, so feel free to take a look.

I also want to make a plea to trans folks to stop using Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and Twitter. The owners of these sites are [openly hostile][zuck] [to trans folks][musk], and in the past have [given information to law enforcement without a signed warrant][no-warrant]. The same goes for Amazon smarthome devices, including the Ring security camera ecosystem, and the Google Nest ecosystem. Be incredibly careful about what you post online; even if you don't do so under your real name, you can still be identified in a variety of ways if you aren't particularly well-versed in preserving your internet anonymity. Most [VPN providers are a scam whose goal is to harvest your data][vpn-scam], and even the single reputable one (Mullvad) isn't a magic bullet for privacy or anonymity. Maintain a variety of aliases, be careful what you post, try to avoid links between accounts or shared passwords, and do your best to mask your IP and device fingerprint.

We don't know how bad this will all get, but if the various histories of resistance to fascism can teach us anything, it's that the people who are actively being targeted by the regime need to lay fucking low. Survival is by itself an act of resistance. Violence should be reserved for self-defense; anything else risks losing the optics/legitimacy game. That kid in New York didn't accomplish diddly shit with his little CEO stunt, and doing anything similar is an act of self-serving catharsis and moral narcissism.

Whatever you feel about gun control in the United States, it's obvious that it's not going to change for the better any time soon. In the meantime, it doesn't hurt to be capable of defending yourself, and at the moment firearms are the most effective available means of self-defense. Regardless of if you are trans, Jewish, a pro-Palestine activist, Latino, a woman, a government employee, or any other category of human currently in the crosshairs of extremists, and provided that your mental health permits it, please considering learning to defend yourself with a firearm. Your continued existence is victory.
